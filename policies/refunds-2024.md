# Standard refund window

Customers on a monthly or annual Relay subscription may request a refund within **14 calendar days of the original purchase**. The window is measured from the timestamp of the initial charge, not from the date a workspace is first used.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method. If the original payment method is no longer available, Finance Operations may issue account credit instead.

# Renewal charges

A renewal charge is normally non-refundable after it has settled. Support may submit an exception request when the customer contacted Northstar within seven days of the renewal and can show that the workspace was not used after renewal.

# Processing an approved refund

Support records the request in the billing queue with the workspace ID, charge ID, customer reason, and the date of the last workspace activity. Finance Operations reviews the request within three business days. Approved card refunds usually appear within five to ten business days after approval.

# Exceptions

Support may not approve an exception independently. Requests involving an amount over $1,000, suspected account compromise, or repeated refund requests must be escalated to Finance Operations.
