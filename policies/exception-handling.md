# What counts as an exception

An exception is a request for treatment outside a published policy, including a refund after the applicable deadline, an early cancellation, a retention pause, or access to a restricted account action.

# Support responsibilities

Support may explain the published rule and collect the required facts, but Support may not promise that an exception will be approved. Every exception request must include the workspace ID, customer account, requested outcome, relevant dates, and supporting evidence.

# Approval routing

Billing exceptions go to Finance Operations. Data-retention exceptions and legal holds go to Security and Privacy. Access or permission exceptions go to the Support Engineering escalation queue. If a request involves multiple categories, Support routes it to the first responsible owner and links the related tickets.

# Urgent requests

Suspected account compromise, active data exposure, or a customer unable to access a time-critical export is urgent. Support follows the incident process in addition to opening the normal exception ticket.

# Communication standard

Until the owner approves the request, Support should use language such as “we have submitted this for review.” Support must not state that a refund, deletion pause, access change, or credit has been approved based only on a submitted ticket.
