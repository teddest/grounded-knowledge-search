# Document ownership

Every operational document has one accountable owner, a status, a version, and an effective date. The owner reviews the document at least every six months and whenever the underlying process changes.

# Current versus archived documents

A document marked `current` is the default source for present-day operational questions. A document marked `archived` remains searchable for historical questions but should not be used to answer a current-policy question when a newer document exists.

# Conflicting guidance

When two current documents appear to conflict, Support records the conflict and asks the accountable owners to resolve it. Until resolved, Support should identify the conflict to the customer rather than silently choosing the more convenient rule.

# Searchability

Owners should use descriptive titles, stable section headings, and the vocabulary customers and agents are likely to use. Important exceptions should be stated explicitly rather than implied by examples.

# Change records

A policy revision must state what changed, why it changed, and its effective date. Material changes should link to the previous version so reviewers can compare the rules.
