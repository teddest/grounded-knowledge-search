# Standard refund window

Customers on a monthly Relay subscription may request a refund within **14 calendar days of the original purchase**. Customers on an annual subscription may request a refund within **30 calendar days of the original purchase**. The window is measured from the original charge timestamp.

A renewal is treated as a new charge for purposes of the standard window only when the customer changes from monthly to annual billing at renewal. Ordinary recurring renewals remain subject to the renewal rules below.

# Renewal charges

Ordinary renewal charges are non-refundable after settlement. Support may submit an exception request when the customer contacts Northstar within seven days of the renewal and the workspace shows no activity after the renewal date.

# Processing an approved refund

Support must record the workspace ID, charge ID, subscription plan, customer reason, and date of the last workspace activity in the billing queue. Finance Operations reviews complete requests within three business days. Approved card refunds generally appear within five to ten business days after approval.

# Exceptions and escalation

Support cannot approve an exception independently. Escalate requests involving more than $1,000, suspected account compromise, repeated refund requests, or a request outside the standard window. Finance Operations may approve an account credit instead of a payment-method refund when returning funds to the original method is not possible.

# Current status

This is the current refund policy as of its effective date. If another document conflicts with this policy, use this version for transactions dated on or after 2025-02-01 and notify the Finance Operations owner about the conflict.
