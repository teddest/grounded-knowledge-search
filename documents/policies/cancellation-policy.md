# Customer cancellation

A workspace administrator can cancel a Relay subscription from **Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period unless Finance Operations approves an earlier termination.

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically delete the workspace or erase historical records. The workspace changes to read-only status when the billing period ends. Administrators can export eligible workspace data before deletion.

# Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation is separate from a refund request. Customers who cancel within the applicable refund window must still submit a refund request through Support. Support should use the current Refund and Credit Policy to determine whether the request is eligible.

# Failed cancellation attempts

If the cancellation control is unavailable, Support records the workspace ID and billing account ID and opens a billing queue ticket. Support must not promise that a cancellation or refund has been completed until the billing queue confirms it.

# Reactivation

A workspace administrator can reactivate a cancelled subscription before the billing period ends. After the workspace enters read-only status, reactivation requires Finance Operations approval and may require a new subscription order.
