# Before kickoff

The Customer Success Manager confirms the customer segment, implementation owner, target launch date, workspace owner, and required data sources. The team records these details in the onboarding tracker before the kickoff meeting.

# Kickoff

The kickoff covers workspace roles, data-import expectations, security contacts, support channels, and the customer's success criteria. The workspace owner must identify at least one backup administrator before production data is imported.

# Data import

Customer Success validates a sample import before requesting a full import. The sample must use non-sensitive or approved test data. If the import contains unexpected personal data, the team pauses the import and contacts Security and Privacy.

# Readiness review

Before launch, the implementation owner confirms that required roles are assigned, the first export has been tested, support contacts are documented, and the customer understands the retention and deletion behavior for cancelled workspaces.

# Handoff

Customer Success attaches the completed readiness checklist to the customer record and introduces the customer to Support. Support owns routine product questions after handoff. Product defects and repeated export failures go to Support Engineering.
