# When will a refund appear?

After Finance Operations approves a card refund, it generally appears on the original payment method within five to ten business days. The timing is controlled partly by the payment provider.

# Does cancelling automatically refund the customer?

No. Cancellation and refund requests are separate. The customer must meet the applicable rules in the current Refund and Credit Policy and submit the request through Support.

# What if a customer says the payment failed?

Confirm the invoice status in Relay and ask the customer to check the billing email. Do not ask for a full card number. If the bank shows a successful charge while Relay shows a failed payment, follow the Support Escalation Procedure.

# Can Support approve a late refund?

Support can collect the facts and submit an exception request but cannot approve an exception. Avoid promising an outcome before Finance Operations reviews it.

# Which policy is current?

For transactions dated on or after February 1, 2025, use Refund and Credit Policy version 2025.1. The 2024 policy is retained for historical questions.
